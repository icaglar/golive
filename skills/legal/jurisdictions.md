# Jurisdiction modules

Add the rows of every jurisdiction where the company is established or where it has users. Thresholds and details change: each row says what to check, and the lawyer confirms. A user in a jurisdiction not listed here gets the common checklist plus a note to check local law.

| Jurisdiction | Data protection | Cookies and marketing | Online sales and site identity | Registrations and roles |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **EU / EEA** | GDPR: privacy notice (Art. 13/14), legal basis per purpose, records of processing, DPIA for high-risk processing, breach notice to the authority within 72 h | ePrivacy: prior opt-in for non-essential cookies; marketing e-mail needs consent (or soft opt-in for existing customers) | Consumer law: pre-contract information, 14-day right of withdrawal; an imprint (Impressum) in Germany and Austria; European Accessibility Act for many consumer e-commerce services | DPO only in some cases; an EU representative (Art. 27) if the company is outside the EU but targets EU users; transfers outside the EEA: adequacy (e.g. the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for certified US companies) or Standard Contractual Clauses |
| **United Kingdom** | UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 (mirrors GDPR) | PECR: consent for non-essential cookies and for most marketing e-mail/SMS | Consumer Contracts Regulations: information and 14-day cancellation | Data protection fee to the ICO for most controllers; UK representative if outside the UK; transfers: UK adequacy, IDTA or the UK addendum to the SCCs |
| **United States** | No federal privacy law: state laws (California CCPA/CPRA and others such as Virginia, Colorado, Texas) apply above their thresholds: notice at collection, "Do Not Sell or Share", honour Global Privacy Control | CAN-SPAM for commercial e-mail: unsubscribe that works within 10 business days, a postal address, honest headers and subject lines; TCPA for SMS (prior express consent) | State consumer and auto-renewal laws for subscriptions; ADA accessibility claims (WCAG 2.1 AA) | COPPA if the service targets children under 13 |
| **Canada** | PIPEDA (and Quebec Law 25: privacy officer, impact assessments, consent for cookies that identify users) | CASL: express or implied consent for commercial electronic messages, identification and unsubscribe in each | Provincial consumer protection for online sales | Privacy officer contact published |
| **Brazil** | LGPD: privacy notice, legal bases, data subject rights, ANPD breach notice | Consent for non-essential cookies per ANPD guidance | Consumer Defense Code: 7-day right of withdrawal for online purchases | A data protection officer (encarregado), with simplifications for small agents |
| **Turkey** | KVKK: aydınlatma metni (md. 10), açık rıza only where needed and separate from it, breach notice to the Board within 72 h | Çerez: açık rıza for non-essential cookies (KVKK cookie guide); commercial e-mail/SMS: İYS registration and consents recorded in İYS, opt-out in every message | 6563: seller identity on the site; ETBİS registration; ön bilgilendirme formu and mesafeli satış sözleşmesi with cayma hakkı | Transfers abroad (KVKK md. 9, amended 2024): usually the Board's standard contract, notified to the KVKK within 5 business days; VERBİS registration above the Board's thresholds; 5651 duties if users publish content |
| **Other** | Check whether a data protection law applies (many follow the GDPR model: e.g. Switzerland nFADP, India DPDP Act, Australia Privacy Act, Japan APPI, South Africa POPIA) | Check cookie and marketing consent rules | Check consumer and e-commerce rules | Check registration duties |

## Data residency

`profile.data_residency` (eu, uk, us, tr, any, or a region) is a project choice, not a legal conclusion: it decides where servers, backups, mail and monitoring are placed, and every processor outside it goes into the transfers table in `legal/processors.md` with its mechanism. Check the current rules of each jurisdiction with the lawyer.
